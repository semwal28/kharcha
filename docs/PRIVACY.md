Kharcha helps you track what you spend and suggests ways to save. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | When you create an account | To sign you in with a one-time code |
| Expenses you enter (amount, category, note, date, payment method) | When you add or edit them | To show your spending, budgets and savings tips |
| Categories and budgets you create | When you create them | Same as above |
| Monthly income (optional) | If you enter it | To show how much you have left |
| AI requests | When you tap "Build my plan" or ask a question | To count your daily AI limit |

We do **not** read your SMS, contacts, bank accounts, location or photos. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or rent your data.

## Using the app without an account

If you use Kharcha without signing in, everything stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## AI features

When you ask for a savings plan or ask a question, the app sends a summary of your recent spending to our server, which sends it to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to write the answer. The summary includes your expenses for the current and previous month, but not your email address. We do not store the AI's answers. We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, expenses, categories, budgets and AI usage records are deleted from our database straight away.
