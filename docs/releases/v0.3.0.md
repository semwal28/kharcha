**Try it in your browser:** https://semwal28.github.io/kharcha/

## Which file do I download?

- **spendwell-0.3.0-live.apk**: sign in with an email code; your data is saved to your account. You can also tap "Use without an account".
- **spendwell-0.3.0.apk**: no account at all; everything stays on the phone. Starts with example data.

On Android, open the file and allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.

If you installed an earlier test build, uninstall it first: this one is signed with a different test key, so Android won't install it on top. With the sign-in version your data is in your account, so nothing is lost.

## New in 0.3.0

- **Savings goals** with progress rings
- **Split a bill:** track what friends owe you, tick it off when they pay
- **Repeats monthly:** rent and subscriptions are added for you each month
- **Budget alerts** at 80% and 100%
- **Monthly report** you can share as a picture
- **Export** to CSV or PDF
- **Paste a bank SMS** to fill in an expense
- **Scan a receipt** (read on the phone, nothing uploaded)
- **Any currency**, picked from your region
- **App lock** with fingerprint, face or PIN
- **Home screen widget**: "Safe to spend today"
- **Use without an account**
- **Web app** on GitHub Pages

## Known limits

- Bill splits are tracked only by you; friends don't get a copy yet.
- Bank SMS is copy and paste, not read automatically.
- Budget alerts show while the app is open.
- The AI coach is off in these builds.
- These test builds are not signed with a Play Store key. When the app goes on Google Play, uninstall this version first.
- The APKs are for phones (ARM). On a computer or Chromebook, use the web app.
