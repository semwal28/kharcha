**Try it in your browser:** https://semwal28.github.io/spendwell/

## Which file do I download?

- **spendwell-0.4.0-live.apk**: sign in with an email code; your data is saved to your account. You can also tap "Use without an account".
- **spendwell-0.4.0.apk**: no account at all; everything stays on the phone. Starts empty.

On Android, open the file and allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.

Already on 0.3.4? This installs straight over it. On an older version, uninstall it once first (older test builds used a different signing key).

## New in 0.4.0

- **Free AI coach.** Ask questions and get a savings plan from a free AI model (Groq or Google Gemini free tier) once it's set up on the server ([docs/AI_COACH.md](https://github.com/semwal28/spendwell/blob/main/docs/AI_COACH.md)). Until then, the app answers on your phone instead of showing an error.
- **New look.** Violet and mango colours, a colour for every category, a greeting, and a 🔥 streak for days in a row you've logged spending.
- **iPhone groundwork.** The iOS app now builds in CI; the plan is in [docs/IOS.md](https://github.com/semwal28/spendwell/blob/main/docs/IOS.md).

## New in 0.3.4

## New in 0.3.0

- **Savings goals** with progress rings
- **Split a bill:** track what friends owe you, tick it off when they pay
- **Repeats monthly:** rent and subscriptions are added for you each month
- **Budget alerts** at 80% and 100%
- **Monthly report** you can share as a picture
- **Export** to CSV or PDF
- **Paste a bank SMS** to fill in an expense
- **Scan a receipt** (read on the phone, nothing uploaded)
- **Any currency**, picked from your region
- **App lock** with fingerprint, face or PIN
- **Home screen widget**: "Safe to spend today"
- **Use without an account**
- **Web app** on GitHub Pages

## Known limits

- Bill splits are tracked only by you; friends don't get a copy yet.
- Bank SMS is copy and paste, not read automatically.
- Budget alerts show while the app is open.
- The AI coach is off in these builds.
- These test builds are not signed with a Play Store key. When the app goes on Google Play, uninstall this version first.
